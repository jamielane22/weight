How these numbers are worked out

Every figure comes from the Morning Weight column of the Tracker tab in the Weigh Ins Google Sheet. The 7-day average is the mean of the weigh-ins in the seven days up to each date; days with no weigh-in are skipped, so a gap doesn't pull the average down. Changes use the 7-day average. Weight lost in the previous 7 days compares today's 7-day average with the last one at least 7 days earlier. Weeks to goal divides the kg still to lose by the average weekly drop in the 7-day average over the last 4 weeks (measured from the last average at least 28 days earlier); it only gives a number when that average is falling. The line breaks where there's more than a week without a weigh-in.
